# Threat Intelligence

This section documents practical cyber-threat-intelligence projects completed during my Blue Team Level 1 training.

The projects focus on turning public reporting and technical evidence into intelligence that supports SOC monitoring, threat hunting, detection engineering, and defensive decision-making.

## Projects

### Financial-Sector Threat Landscape

I am developing a threat-intelligence assessment of actors relevant to European financial services, with an Eastern European focus. The project begins with an APT41 deep dive and will compare APT41 with Cobalt Group, Silence, and APT38.

The assessment includes actor profiles, source validation, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, a comparison dataset, intelligence gaps, and financial-sector detection recommendations.
