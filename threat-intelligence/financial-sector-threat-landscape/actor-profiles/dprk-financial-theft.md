# DPRK Financial-Theft Activity

## Status

**Research not started**

## Project Relevance

This profile will examine North Korean financial-theft activity affecting banks, payment systems, cryptocurrency exchanges, bridges and other virtual-asset infrastructure.

Public labels such as APT38, BlueNoroff, TraderTraitor, Lazarus Group, BeagleBoyz and related names will be mapped source by source. They will not be treated as exact synonyms unless the cited source explicitly makes that connection.

## Intelligence Questions

1. Which source-specific DPRK clusters are directly linked to banks, SWIFT, payment systems or cryptocurrency theft during 2023–2026?
2. Which initial-access methods are used against financial and virtual-asset organisations?
3. How are cloud services, developer environments, blockchain infrastructure and social engineering involved?
4. How are stolen assets laundered or moved?
5. Which identity, endpoint, cloud and blockchain data sources support detection?
6. Which claims are confirmed, assessed or uncertain?
